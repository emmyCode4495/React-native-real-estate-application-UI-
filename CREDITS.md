# Photo credits

The listing, hero and interior photographs are hosted by [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), including commercial use. Thank you to the photographers below.

The app loads them from `images.unsplash.com` at runtime (hotlinking, which Unsplash allows). If a photo cannot load, the app falls back to the built-in illustration for that home.

Each photo's page ID lets you check its credit and license: `https://unsplash.com/photos/<page id>`.
